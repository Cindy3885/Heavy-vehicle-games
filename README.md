# Heavy Vehicle Games
### Interactive Games & Industry-Specific Learning Experiences

An independent collection of original, AI-assisted browser game projects inspired by the heavy-vehicle repair, recovery, workshop and parts industry.

These projects explore how industry knowledge, creative direction, problem-solving and AI-assisted development can be combined to create interactive digital experiences.

Each game started with an original concept and detailed creative and functional direction, covering the intended gameplay, visual presentation, interactions, features and overall player experience.

---

## 🎮 Game Collection

### 1. GP Truck Aid – Recovery Run
**A Heavy-Vehicle Recovery & Safety Awareness Game**

An arcade-style recovery game based on the process of recovering a disabled commercial vehicle and safely delivering it to a repair workshop.

The concept combines heavy-vehicle recovery scenarios with road safety, workshop awareness and incident prevention.

**Gameplay and learning themes:**
- Heavy-vehicle recovery and towing.
- Road and workshop hazard awareness.
- Pedestrian and forklift safety.
- Collision prevention and vehicle control.
- Near-miss awareness and incident reporting.
- Workshop yard navigation and loading bay manoeuvring.

## 💡 Concept Creation, Creative Direction & Development

These projects demonstrate more than the use of AI to generate code. The original concepts, industry-specific ideas and creative direction were developed by me.

My contribution included:

- **Original concept development:** Identifying opportunities to turn industry-related knowledge and everyday activities into interactive game experiences.
- **Creative direction:** Defining the visual style, atmosphere, game environments, sounds, interactions and overall player experience.
- **Functional requirements:** Describing how each game should operate, including gameplay mechanics, controls, features, feedback and game states.
- **Industry knowledge:** Applying practical familiarity with heavy-vehicle repair, recovery operations, workshop environments and parts identification.
- **Learning and safety design:** Incorporating industry-related terminology, hazards, safety awareness and educational elements into the game concepts.
- **AI instruction and collaboration:** Preparing detailed prompts and specifications to guide AI-assisted development.
- **Testing and refinement:** Playing the games, reviewing the results and identifying areas requiring improvement.

AI tools assisted with translating the concepts and specifications into browser-based game implementations. The creative concepts, requirements and direction were human-led.

## Purpose

This collection demonstrates how practical industry knowledge, creative thinking and AI-assisted digital development can be applied to create engaging, accessible and industry-specific interactive experiences.

The projects are experimental educational and entertainment experiences, not substitutes for certified driving instruction, formal workplace safety training or professional technical training.
