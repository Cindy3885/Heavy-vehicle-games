COLLECT 20 PARTS TO UNLOCK THE BONUS ROUND!

Eight workshop hazards are present from the start: two workshop personnel and six different physical hazards. After collecting your 14th part, a ninth hazard enters the workshop!

The bonus round lasts 60 seconds. Collect as many extra parts as possible while the workshop goes into frenzy mode!

Avoid workshop staff, forklifts, oil spills, compressed-air hoses, shelving units, loose tyres, engine components, pallet jacks and your own trail.
