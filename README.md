# Heavy Vehicle Games
### Interactive Games & Learning Experiences

A collection of original, AI-assisted browser games inspired by the heavy-vehicle repair, recovery and workshop industry.

These projects combine creative concept development, industry-specific knowledge, interactive gameplay and educational content to turn practical workplace scenarios into engaging digital experiences.

The games explore different aspects of the industry, from vehicle recovery and workshop safety to heavy-vehicle parts identification and technical terminology.

---

## 🎮 Game Collection

### 1. GP Truck Aid – Recovery Run

**A Safe Recovery Is a Successful Recovery.**

An arcade-style heavy-vehicle recovery game in which players operate a recovery truck towing a disabled commercial vehicle through a series of road and workshop environments.

The objective is to complete a safe recovery, navigate the workshop yard and deliver the disabled vehicle to its designated loading bay.

**Key Features & Learning Objectives:**
- Heavy-vehicle recovery and towing awareness.
- Road hazard identification and collision prevention.
- Pedestrian and forklift safety.
- Oil spill awareness and near-miss reporting.
- Workplace incident response and corrective actions.
- Controlled vehicle manoeuvring and loading bay alignment.

**Status:** Development and testing.

### 3. Heavy Vehicle Parts Word Game

An interactive word-guessing game designed around heavy-vehicle components, systems and technical terminology.

Players solve clue-based questions by identifying missing letters. Each correct answer unlocks another section of a heavy truck illustration, progressively assembling the complete vehicle as the game advances.

**Learning Objectives:**
- Heavy-vehicle component identification.
- Technical terminology recognition.
- Familiarity with vehicle systems and their functions.
- Knowledge reinforcement through interactive gameplay.

**Status:** Development and testing.

## 💡 Concept & Development

Each game begins with an original concept inspired by practical industry knowledge, educational opportunities or familiar workplace scenarios.

Development involves:

- Identifying a concept and defining its purpose.
- Designing gameplay mechanics and player objectives.
- Translating industry knowledge into interactive scenarios.
- Specifying visual design, functionality and user experience.
- Defining educational elements and safety-related content.
- Using AI-assisted development to translate detailed requirements into working browser applications.
- Testing gameplay, functionality, usability and responsiveness.

The projects demonstrate how practical industry experience, creative thinking and AI-assisted development can be combined to produce accessible interactive learning experiences.

## 🛠️ Technology & Platform

The games are developed as browser-based applications, using technologies such as:

- HTML5
- CSS
- JavaScript
- HTML5 Canvas API (where applicable)
- Responsive interface design
- GitHub Pages for online demonstrations

The individual project implementations and technologies may differ.

## 🎯 Purpose

The objective of this collection is to explore creative ways of making heavy-vehicle industry knowledge more accessible and engaging through interactive digital experiences.

These games are educational and demonstration projects. They are not substitutes for formal technical training, certified driving instruction or workplace safety training.
