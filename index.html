Behind the Games

These projects began with original concepts and creative direction inspired by the heavy-vehicle repair, recovery and workshop industry.

My contribution included:

  • Original game concepts and creative direction.
  • Defining gameplay, features, interactions and visual style.
  • Developing detailed functional requirements and instructions.
  • Applying practical heavy-vehicle industry knowledge.
  • Incorporating safety awareness and learning themes.
  • AI-assisted development, testing and refinement.

AI tools assisted with the coding and implementation. Acode was also used as part of the mobile development and testing workflow.
